The builder first works out the fewest pallets the hard limits allow (weight, height, volume), then fits everything into that many, relaxing the soft rules only as far as needed: strict → load caps relaxed → light tip-able leftovers become hand-fit filler → creative stacking. Anything relaxed is flagged on the pallet. Set a pallet target in Settings to force a count. Rules applied: full layers of one box group first, sorted by layer weight; leftovers combined into flat mixed layers only where box heights are within the tolerance; weak boxes never share a layer; base boxes (heavy, arrive palletized) stay on the deck, are kept stacked together as received (optionally capped with stack:N layers), and get labeled where they sit; fragile layers only carry layers under the fragile limit; layers of light boxes (under the light-box weight) carry at most the load cap above them, or a load: lb value set per group; sturdy boxes are limited only by the pallet weight; height and weight limits checked as each layer is added. Same-height fill is estimated by floor area, so treat mixed-layer counts as approximate until a saved pattern replaces them.
